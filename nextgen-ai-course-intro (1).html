What you can do after 28 days

Concrete, demonstrable competencies — not vague "understanding of ML concepts".

🗂️

Clean Any Dataset

Load, clean, and feature-engineer any real-world tabular dataset — handling missing values, outliers, lag features, and categorical encodings with confidence.
